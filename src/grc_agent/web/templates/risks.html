How scores are set

  • Risk = likelihood × impact, each 1 (rare, negligible) to 5 (almost certain, severe).
  • Starting likelihood: a gap is likely (4); an open item or a warning is possible (3); a failed connector check is likely (4).
  • Starting impact follows the obligation's severity: critical 5, high 4, medium 3, low 2.
  • Levels: 1–5 low, 6–11 medium, 12–19 high, 20–25 critical.
  • Treatments: mitigate (fix it), accept (needs a written reason), transfer (contract or insurance), avoid (stop the processing).

Risks come from the findings and connector checks, so they update when those change. Your edits are kept.
