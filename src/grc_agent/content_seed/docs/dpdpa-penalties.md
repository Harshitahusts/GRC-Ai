DPDPA penalties go up to ₹250 crore per instance. The **Data Protection Board of India** enforces the Act. It can inquire into breaches and impose **monetary penalties** set out in the **Schedule** to the Act.

## Penalty schedule

| Breach | Maximum penalty |
|---|---|
| Failing to take reasonable security safeguards to prevent a personal data breach (Section 8(5)) | ₹250 crore |
| Failing to inform the Board or affected people of a personal data breach (Section 8(6)) | ₹200 crore |
| Breaching the additional obligations for children (Section 9) | ₹200 crore |
| Breaching the additional obligations of a Significant Data Fiduciary (Section 10) | ₹150 crore |
| A Data Principal breaching their duties (Section 15) | ₹10,000 |
| Breaching a voluntary undertaking accepted by the Board (Section 32) | Up to the penalty for the original breach |
| Breaching any other provision of the Act or Rules | ₹50 crore |

## How the Board decides (Section 33)

The Board considers matters including:

- the nature, gravity and duration of the breach
- the type and nature of the personal data affected
- whether it was repetitive
- whether the organisation gained or avoided a loss because of it
- what was done to mitigate its effects, and how promptly
- whether the penalty is proportionate and effective, and its likely impact on the organisation

A well-documented compliance programme and a fast, honest breach response count in your favour.

## How the Board works

- The Board acts on a **complaint**, a **reference** from the government, a court direction, or a breach intimation (Section 27).
- It works as a **digital office**, with proceedings conducted digitally (Section 28).
- It can accept a **voluntary undertaking** from the organisation to take specific action, which bars proceedings on the same matter (Section 32).
- It can direct **mediation** where appropriate (Section 31).

## Appeals (Section 29)

Anyone aggrieved by a Board decision can appeal to the **Telecom Disputes Settlement and Appellate Tribunal (TDSAT)** within **60 days**.
