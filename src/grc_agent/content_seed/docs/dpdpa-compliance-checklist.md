A practical DPDPA compliance checklist for getting a business ready for the DPDPA and the DPDP Rules. Each item links to the doc that explains it.

## 1. Know your data

- [ ] List the personal data you collect about customers, users and employees.
- [ ] Record why you use each type, where it's stored, and who can access it.
- [ ] List every tool and vendor that stores or handles it, and where they store it.
- [ ] Find out whether any users could be under 18.

## 2. Lawful basis, notice and consent

- [ ] Choose a lawful basis for each purpose: consent or a specific legitimate use. See [consent](/docs/dpdpa-consent-and-legitimate-uses).
- [ ] Publish a [notice](/docs/dpdpa-notice-requirements) at every collection point.
- [ ] Replace pre-ticked or bundled consent with a clear, per-purpose opt-in.
- [ ] Make withdrawing consent as easy as giving it.
- [ ] Keep records of the notice shown and the consent given.

## 3. Security and breaches

- [ ] Put [reasonable security safeguards](/docs/dpdpa-data-fiduciary-obligations) in place: access control, encryption, logging, backups.
- [ ] Write a [breach playbook](/docs/dpdpa-personal-data-breach) with Board and user notification templates.
- [ ] Keep a breach log.

## 4. Retention and erasure

- [ ] Set a retention period for each type of personal data.
- [ ] Delete data when the purpose is served or consent is withdrawn, unless a law requires keeping it.
- [ ] Make your vendors delete it too.

## 5. Rights and grievances

- [ ] Publish a contact for data questions.
- [ ] Set up a way to request [access, correction and erasure](/docs/dpdpa-data-principal-rights), and to raise grievances.
- [ ] Log every request and response, with dates.

## 6. Vendors and transfers

- [ ] Sign a data processing agreement with every vendor that handles personal data.
- [ ] Check [cross-border transfers](/docs/dpdpa-cross-border-data-transfer) against government restrictions and sector rules.

## 7. Children

- [ ] If any users may be under 18, add age checks and [verifiable parental consent](/docs/dpdpa-childrens-data).
- [ ] Turn off tracking and targeted ads for children.

## 8. Governance

- [ ] Name an owner for data protection.
- [ ] Train staff who handle personal data.
- [ ] Review the programme at least once a year, and whenever the Rules change.
- [ ] If you could be a [Significant Data Fiduciary](/docs/dpdpa-significant-data-fiduciary), plan for a DPO, DPIA and audits.

Know the stakes: [DPDPA penalties](/docs/dpdpa-penalties) reach ₹250 crore.
